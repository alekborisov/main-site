Intro

This is the personal site of artist, philosopher, and web3 enthusiast Alek Borisov.
Here you can find links to my projects, digital graphics, and texts.

Home > Intro


The main focus of my artistic and philosophical interest is human ontology and culture in the context of new technologies. I see the transformation of the body and consciousness through their fusion with digital substance as reminiscent of the alchemists’ search for a philosophical elixir. At the same time, the progress of science and technology affects not only the individual but also their environment, shaping the post-digital ecosystem of Earth and the Solar System. On the other side, the vector of exploration of new territories is shifting toward the space of imagination, expressed in the creation and development of Metaverse, brain–computer interfaces, and artificial neural networks. The closest and most immediate sphere of implementation of these processes is the Internet and its modern frontier — Web3 infrastructure.
